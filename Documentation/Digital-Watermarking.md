# Digital Watermarking Report

## Purpose
Digital watermarking helps identify ownership of digital media assets.

## Assets That Can Be Watermarked
- Images and textures
- Artwork and concept designs
- Preview renders and promotional videos

## Types of Watermarks
1. Visible watermark: a logo or text displayed on an image.
2. Invisible watermark: information embedded in media that may not be immediately visible.

## Proposed Implementation
- Add a project name or creator credit to preview images.
- Keep original high-quality assets in secure storage.
- Maintain records of asset ownership and creation dates.
- Use watermarked previews when sharing work publicly.

## Limitations
Watermarks do not completely prevent copying or unauthorized use.

## Status
This report describes the proposed watermarking process. Actual watermarking has not yet been performed.
